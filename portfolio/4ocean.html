Context & Background

4ocean is a mission-driven organization operating at the intersection of environmental impact and global commerce. As the organization scaled, its digital platform struggled to keep up with growing operational and communication needs.

The existing setup was built incrementally over time, resulting in fragmented user journeys, limited flexibility, and increasing maintenance overhead. While the mission remained strong, the digital experience no longer reflected the scale, clarity, and credibility of the organization

Proposed Solution

A Digital Collection Application (DCA) for field data entry

A centralized database for automated synchronization

A real-time operational dashboard for monitoring and analytics

Rule-based access controls for Team, Admin, and Investors

Workflow & Transformation Matrix

Role 01

Cleanup Team

Responsibility: Collect and record daily waste collection data in the field.

Before:

Operations team manually recorded daily collection results on paper. Manual recording of each bag leads to inconsistent data and delay in verification. Time-consuming reporting process after field operations with no real-time visibility of total team progress.

After (Digital Collection Application):

Each team member inputs data directly into the Digital Collection Application (DCA). Data is automatically synced to a centralized system (One bag, one photo) ensuring transparent weight verification and proper process documentation. Real-time visibility of team performance.

Role 02

4ocean Admin

Responsibility: Get data from Cleanup Team, monitor, validate, and report operational performance data.

Before:

Data aggregation required up to two weeks before presenting. Limited ability to monitor daily operational performance.

After:

Real-time dashboard provides operational metrics 90% faster. Faster validation and reporting process.

Role 03

Investors

Responsibility: Track environmental impact and operational transparency.

Before:

Impact data was only available after manual summaries.

After:

Access to a real-time read-only impact dashboard.
