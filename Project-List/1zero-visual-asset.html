Project Scope

Beyond the website itself, 1zero needed a consistent set of ready-to-use visual assets for everyday business use — covering email communication, professional networking, presentations, and printed collateral.

Each asset was designed to feel consistent with 1zero's existing brand identity — color, logo, and typography — while remaining practical and easy for the team to use day-to-day.

Some assets, such as the personal LinkedIn cover, were built as reusable templates so every team member could apply their own name, title, and highlights without starting from scratch.
