Project Scope

As part of 1zero's content and marketing activities, I explored how AI could support different stages of the creative production process.

Instead of relying on raw AI-generated outputs, I used AI as a creative assistant for ideation, content generation, visual exploration, and copy development.

Each output was then reviewed, selected, refined, and aligned with 1zero's visual identity and communication goals before being used as final content.

Outcome

Turning AI into a practical creative workflow.

The process helped accelerate content exploration while maintaining creative control and consistency with 1zero's brand direction.

Key Takeaway

AI works best as a creative partner — not as a replacement for creative judgement.

Key Learnings

Same Prompt, Different Results

The same prompt can produce different outputs each time. This taught me that AI generation requires exploration, evaluation, and selection rather than expecting one predictable result.

Be Specific When Refining

When refining an output, instructions need to be specific about what should change and where. I also learned to clearly tell AI what should remain untouched to avoid unwanted changes.

AI Needs Clear Direction

The more precise the direction, the more controlled the output. I learned to be strict and intentional with prompts, treating AI as a creative tool that still requires human judgement and direction.
