Project Scope

A consulting website needs to communicate trust, expertise, and value quickly. Visitors should easily understand what the company does, what services are available, and why they should consider working with the company.

The previous website contained valuable information, but the hierarchy, service communication, and contact journey could be clearer and more intentional.

The redesign focused on creating a more modern and professional digital presence while making the website easier to navigate, understand, and engage with.

Context & Background

1zero is a technology and business consulting company that helps other organisations with digitalisation, technology, and business transformation.

Why the Website Was Redesigned

The previous website had several limitations that made it difficult for visitors to clearly understand 1zero:

  • Outdated visual style
  • Services were not clearly presented or prioritised
  • Information hierarchy was too abstract and included unnecessary content
  • The process of working with 1zero was unclear
  • No portfolio or project showcase
  • No testimonials or social proof
  • The team behind the company was not introduced
  • Company activities and events were not showcased

SEO & Technical Optimisation

Alongside the visual redesign, I also considered the website's technical foundation to ensure that the implementation supported accessibility, responsiveness, semantic structure, and maintainability.

Focus: Establishing a clear semantic heading hierarchy across the website.

Page titles, section headings, and supporting content were structured to make the content easier to understand for both users and search engines.

Focus: Consistent behaviour across desktop, tablet, and mobile screen sizes.

Spacing, typography, content positioning, and component behaviour were refined across responsive breakpoints.

Images were reviewed for appropriate dimensions, compression, loading behaviour, and visual quality to support a more efficient website experience.

Technical considerations were reviewed alongside the visual and responsive implementation to create a more maintainable website foundation.

Implementation Challenges

Translating UI Design into Code

Translating the visual design into a functional website required careful attention to layout, spacing, typography, and component behaviour.

Responsive Behaviour

The design needed to remain consistent across desktop, tablet, and mobile while preserving hierarchy and usability.

Balancing Content & Visual Design

Consulting websites contain a significant amount of information, so the challenge was presenting enough content without overwhelming the visitor.

Maintaining Consistency

Reusable components and design patterns were established to maintain consistency across different pages and sections.

Implementation Feasibility

UI decisions were considered alongside the technical implementation to ensure that the proposed experience could be realistically built and maintained.

Prioritising the Experience

Within the available timeline, the most important improvements were prioritised first, particularly hierarchy, service presentation, responsiveness, and conversion flow.
